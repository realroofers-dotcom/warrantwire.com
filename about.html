What the wire does, and what it does not

Warrant Wire reads the full text of every filing the SEC publishes and records the ones carrying the language of a warrant financing. It tells you who filed, when, and in what form.

It does not tell you what the terms mean. That is a different job and it is done at 8k10q.com, which reads the filing and the exhibit and writes it in plain English.
