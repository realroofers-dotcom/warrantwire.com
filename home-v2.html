Form 424B5 · Prospectus supplementp. S-14

Subject to the terms and conditions set forth herein and in the Securities Purchase Agreement, the Company shall issue to each Purchaser, severally and not jointly, Common Warrants to purchase up to an aggregate number of shares of Common Stock equal to 100% of the shares of Common Stock (including the Pre-Funded Warrant Shares) purchased by such Purchaser hereunder.

If at any time while this Warrant is outstanding the Company sells any Common Stock at a price per share less than the Exercise Price then in effect, the Exercise Price shall be reduced to equal such lower price.

Notwithstanding the foregoing, the Holder shall not have the right to exercise any portion of this Warrant to the extent that after giving effect to such issuance the Holder, together with its Affiliates and Attribution Parties, would beneficially own in excess of the Beneficial Ownership Limitation, which shall be 4.99% of the number of shares outstanding immediately after giving effect to the issuance.

The Company shall not effect any exercise of this Warrant, and a Holder shall not have the right to exercise any portion of this Warrant, pursuant to Section 2 or otherwise, to the extent that the foregoing would result in a Fundamental Transaction as defined herein, subject to the Black Scholes Value calculation set forth in Section 3(e).

In plain English

If the company ever sells stock cheaper, every warrant already out gets repriced down to match — and your stake shrinks to pay for it.

A real clause. Theriva Biologics (TOVX), 424B5. The full read is the report.
