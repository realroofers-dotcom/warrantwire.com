# newsweed.com — what it is, and what we do with it later

*Written 12 Sep 2026 from a conversation with Mark. Not built yet. Read this before touching the site.*

## The one-line

**Real news for the weed audience — not news about weed.**

## The editorial line, in Mark's words

- A **data-driven general news desk**: crime (murders, rape), courts, the economy, jobs, stocks and markets, and self-help that goes *beyond drugs*. "Data data data."
- The **only cannabis coverage** is medical and lifestyle — the good **and the bad** — reported straight.
- Mark does not use weed. The stance is respect, not advocacy. It is not a stoner site and not an industry site.
- The reader is a real person who happens to be comfortable with the name, and who will enjoy real news, not just weed info.

## Why Mark believes in it

- The demographic is large, growing "in leaps and bounds" (legal in more states every cycle), under-served, and *trusts the name*. Nobody else is making a general news desk for them — every cannabis site covers legalisation and product tests.
- It is a **demographic play, not a topic play.**

## The business plan

- **Build the audience, then sell the name.** Use-and-sale: the site runs *and* the listing stays up.
- Listed on wallstdomains.com as a **seven-name package at $900,000**: NEWSWEED.COM + newsweed.co, .net, .org, .us, newsweeds.com, newsweedstocks.com. Siblings carry the package price and say "included, not sold separately."
- Registered **2012** — three years before **newsweed.fr**, an unrelated French cannabis-news site (since 2015, OVH). The .fr owner is the natural first buyer.
- Mark wants to advertise wallstdomains.com through **AdHotBox** (house campaign across the network, newsweed.com included). Not done yet.

## Facts to keep straight

- **Traffic today is ~2 real visits a day** (Cloudflare Web Analytics, 7-day counts ≈ 13 page loads). The HTTP-traffic numbers (4,000+/day from NL/KR/JP/HK) are bots. **Never put a traffic claim in the listing.**
- Core Web Vitals are green; the site loads fine. The problem is distribution, not the build.
- newsweed.com is on Cloudflare (zone `005eec1b85e178d3670b7ed40506cc91`), Google sign-in redirect URI was set up 11 Sep.

## How to build it, when we do

- Treat it as a **second masthead on the warrantwire / 8k10q newsroom machinery** (crime, courts, markets desks already exist) — not a site from scratch.
- Add the desks it needs: jobs, self-help, medical-cannabis (balanced).
- The **JobCreation.us** engine belongs here too: federal contract awards by geography (USAspending.gov API: amount, recipient, NAICS, place of performance to county/ZIP) + open solicitations (SAM.gov API) + an *estimated*, labelled jobs number (BLS employment-per-dollar multipliers by industry). A page per county/ZIP: "$X awarded here this year, to whom, for what, est. N jobs; $Y open for bid now." Feeds the jobs desk on newsweed and warrantwire, and stands alone on jobcreation.us. Open question: who is it for first — worker, contractor, or reader (lean: worker).
- On the site, an "Acquire this brand →" link to the wallstdomains listing, visible, not buried.
