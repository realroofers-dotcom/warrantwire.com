What we look for

Price reset

The exercise price falls if the company later sells stock cheaper. Every cheap issuance makes the existing paper convert into more shares at a lower price.

Cashless exercise

Shares issue without the holder paying the exercise price. The company receives no cash and the shares still reach the market.

Ownership blocker

Usually 4.99%, raisable on 61 days' notice. A holder can own paper convertible into a great deal of a company and never appear in an ownership filing.

Warrant inducement

Existing holders are offered a lower price to exercise early, typically in exchange for new warrants — often more than they exercised.
