The rules the wire is built to

Every figure points at its document.Nothing is typed; everything is computed from a filing, and the filing is one click away.
It says so when it does not know.A missing figure is left out and named as missing. Nothing is estimated.
Computed, not concluded.The verdict names what the record shows — repriced, diluted, split — and never why.
Warrants only.Nothing here evaluates a company’s products, its science or its prospects.
Nobody is anonymous.Everyone who writes here does so under a real name, verified by a telephone call.
Opinion, not advice.Unless the writer is a licensed professional registered with us, and chooses to say so, verdict by verdict.
Dated, never silently changed.Every verdict, pattern and rule carries its date. A change gets a new date under the old one.
Nothing here says anyone did anything wrong.A match is not a finding. A filing appears because it is worth reading.
