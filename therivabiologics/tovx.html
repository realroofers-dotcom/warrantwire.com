Who wrote this and what he owns

Mark Nejmeh, founder of JobCreation.us, publishes the Triggered Short research and built this measurement. He holds shares in Theriva Biologics (TOVX) and has done since before this page existed.

That is why this company is the sample: it is the one whose filings he has read every one of. It is also why the method is published rather than described — so that nothing here rests on trusting him.

Filer information and contact details are on who runs the wire. Any response from anyone named on this page will be published in full and unedited.

What none of this means. A grade measures how a document is written. It is not a finding that any rule was broken — that is the Commission's to decide and nobody else's. Being named on this page is not an allegation of anything. Nothing here is a view on this company, its business or its share price, and nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
