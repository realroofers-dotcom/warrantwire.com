Tell the regulators

File a tip with the SEC.

If you think a company, a broker or anyone in the market broke the securities laws, the SEC takes tips from anyone, online, and it costs nothing. It takes a few minutes.

Go to the SEC tip form

sec.gov/submit-tip-or-complaint · the SEC's own site, opens in a new tab

The first thing the SEC asks

Its form starts by asking what your tip or complaint relates to. These are its four choices, word for word:

Possible securities law violations like fraud, Ponzi schemes, insider trading, market manipulation, or other wrongdoing

A problem I'm having with my investment account or financial professional

The SEC or self-regulatory organizations (SROs) such as stock exchanges or FINRA

An SEC employee

Read off sec.gov on 14 Nov 2026.

Have this ready

  • The company's name and ticker.
  • The dates: when it happened, and when you noticed.
  • What happened, in your own words, short and plain.
  • The filings or papers that show it. A link to the filing on the SEC's own site is best.
  • How the SEC can reach you, if you want them to.

About a broker or a brokerage firm?

FINRA, which oversees brokerage firms, takes its own tips: finra.org → file a tip.

Rather send a letter? Print one here

The SEC says it prefers its online form, and it also takes tips by mail. Fill this in, click Print, sign it and mail it. What you type stays in your own browser; we do not see it.
