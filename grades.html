What a grade is not. It measures how a document is written. It is not a finding that any rule was broken — that is the Commission's to decide and nobody else's. It is not a view on any company, its business or its share price, and it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything. Why a document is written this way is not measured here and is not asserted.
And what the sample is. These are filings that carried warrant financing language, which is what this wire watches. They are not a sample of SEC filings generally and must not be described as one.
